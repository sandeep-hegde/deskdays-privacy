# Deskdays Privacy Policy

This repository hosts the privacy policy for **Deskdays**, a beautiful macOS menubar calendar app with Apple Calendar events, customizable themes, and countdowns.

## View Privacy Policy

**Live URL:** [https://sandeep-hegde.github.io/deskdays-privacy/](https://sandeep-hegde.github.io/deskdays-privacy/)

## Privacy Highlights

Deskdays is built with privacy as a priority:

- **No data collection** - Zero analytics, tracking, or telemetry
- **Local storage only** - All your data stays on your Mac
- **Read-only Calendar access** - Apple Calendar events are read locally only to display them in Deskdays
- **No third-party SDKs** - Pure Apple frameworks only
- **No network calls** - Except Apple's StoreKit (for purchases)
- **Zero servers** - We don't operate any servers to collect data

Your Apple Calendar events, calendar images, countdowns, and settings never leave your device.

## About Deskdays

Deskdays is a native macOS menubar app that provides:

- **Calendar View** - Quick access to monthly calendar from your menubar
- **Apple Calendar Events** - View events from calendars on your Mac
- **Custom Themes** - Choose from preset themes or upload your own photos for each month
- **Countdowns** - Track important dates with beautiful, auto-rotating countdowns
- **Minimal Design** - Clean, distraction-free interface that stays out of your way

**Platform:** macOS 14 Sonoma or later
**Developer:** Sandeep Hegde, Advaya Apps
**Built with:** Swift 6, SwiftUI, StoreKit 2
